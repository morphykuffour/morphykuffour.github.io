It began as the manta under the skulls and was made an include when two more clips wanted the same treatment, on the same reasoning the cascade was -- three copies of a reel and a stepped keyframe is three copies of one idea, and copies drift apart at the first fix. Those want continuous footage -- the three-copy stack is a phase trick, and phase needs something running to be behind. These want the opposite.
