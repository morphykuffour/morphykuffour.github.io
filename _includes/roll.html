{%- comment -%} A strip of pictures run past the page in one endless loop: a roll. It began as the skulls under the rain and was made an include when the frieze wanted the same treatment, on the same reasoning the stop motion and the cascade were -- two copies of a doubled track and a translate keyframe is two copies of one idea, and copies drift apart at the first fix. Parameters: data the list to run, one entry per picture, each with `file`, `width` and `height`. The dimensions are the file's own: these are lazy and sit below the fold, and one without its size reserves the wrong box and shifts the strip sideways when it lands dir folder under /images/ the files sit in ioda optional: set it and every picture takes the theme's dark-mode opt-out. That is for ink on paper -- pencil lines want to be the light half of an inverted page -- and wrong for anything photographic, which wants its own colours back and gets them by taking the page invert twice The strip is written out twice. The loop walks it exactly one pass to the left and starts over, which puts the copy where the original stood -- so there is no jump to hide, and no script is needed to hide one. The pictures carry no alt text of their own, deliberately: a dozen of them each describing a different picture is a dozen interruptions for one idea, and none of them is load-bearing. The caption below the strip says the whole of it once -- the same call the stop motion include makes for its ten frames. It emits the window and the track inside it and nothing else -- no heading, no caption, no section. Those belong to the page, which is where the two rolls on the homepage differ from each other and this file is where they do not. The section around it wants `.roll` on it: that is what the stylesheet hangs the hover pause on, so that reading the caption stops the strip too. {%- endcomment -%}
